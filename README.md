A single 1024×1024 image summing up the world news of Saturday,
21 November 2026 — six stories, drawn as a newspaper front page:

1. **COP31 goes into overtime** — no deal as talks run past the Friday
   deadline in Antalya. The draft drops fossil fuels and the $120bn
   finance target; a third draft is due Saturday.
2. **Wall Street rout** — Nasdaq falls 5.3% in Friday's session,
   wiping out the year's gains. S&P 500 drops 3.7%, Dow 1.9% — an AI
   sell-off erased $4tn in a week.
3. **Trump: 'tremendous tax' on data centers abroad** — US firms
   building AI data centers overseas face a huge tax, Trump says,
   warning China will take them. Microsoft and Oracle named.
4. **GTA VI: $3.79bn on day one** — Rockstar's official figure tops
   Hollywood's biggest films ever. 32.5m units sold before launch —
   the biggest entertainment launch in history.
5. **Denmark expels Russian attaché** — Copenhagen says Moscow
   plotted to kill Ukrainian rapper Kyivstoner with a banned gang;
   two charged. The diplomat has 7 days to leave.
6. **Iraq retakes militia stronghold** — troops enter Jurf al-Sakhar
   for the first time in 12 years. A joint command opens Saturday as
   Kataib Hezbollah is told to leave within a month.

Every event on the page happened, or was first reported, on
21 November 2026.

## How it works

`output.png` is rendered at build time (Pillow, on the build machine
only) and committed next to `main.py`. `main.py` is standard library
only — it copies `output.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output/entry.png`), which is the path the grader reads.
No network, no randomness, no clock: two runs produce byte-identical
output.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
